# Art direction and asset plan

## Visual system

Native 18:9. Obsidian `#101114`, warm white `#f4f1eb`, satin titanium edges and frosted glass. Restrained ivory/amber, cobalt, violet and emerald accents. Orthographic/isometric-feeling layers, low camera elevation and controlled real perspective on evidence surfaces. Typography is clean editorial Inter; evidence remains sharp and captions stay in screen space. Avoid neon grids, human/robot faces, unlabelled fabricated UI and extreme camera angles while reading.

## Reusable DALL·E 3-ready prompts

These are reusable prompts, not a claim that the execution tool exposed a DALL·E 3 model. The actual concepts in this project were generated with the available image tool.

### A — Hero environment

Cinematic 2:1 / 18:9 art-direction keyframe for a premium Vox-style HCI explainer mixed with Apple WWDC precision. Luxurious obsidian and charcoal architectural void, three offset floating frosted-glass slabs with thick polished edges, low satin-black plinth, warm ivory light pool, restrained amber accent and muted cobalt reflection. Slightly isometric orthographic camera. Main slab on right 60 percent, left 35 percent negative space for a headline added later. Soft contact shadows, controlled film grain, physically plausible lighting. No people, text, logos, neon grids or generated UI.

### B — Speaker avatars

Four distinct abstract geometric sculpture avatars, equal visual weight, evenly spaced on separate low obsidian circular plinths. Left to right: ivory ceramic disc stack with suspended amber orb; cobalt interlocking rounded architectural bridge with clear insert; violet triangular prism with two thin transparent measurement rings; emerald protective arch around a frosted sphere. Shared brushed metal, ceramic and frosted-glass materials. Charcoal studio, warm rim light, restrained reflections, slightly isometric view. Wide 2:1 composition with negative space for names added in post. No humans, heads, faces, eyes, limbs, lettering, neon rainbow or shiny toy styling. For production, deliver separate transparent silhouettes with all object parts intact.

### C — Demo theatre

A high-end 2:1 editorial display theatre on an obsidian background. Two large clean landscape floating display frames, narrow thick frosted-glass bezels, satin titanium corners, softly lit plinth below. Slight off-frontal angle; screens remain readable. Warm ivory rim on left, restrained cobalt rim on right, one small amber geometric marker outside the screen. Opaque empty dark screen interiors for real footage compositing. Bottom negative space for captions. No fake UI, text, logos or people. Use as material/lighting reference; final evidence layout must not overlap or obscure either interface.

## Intended editorial flow

18-second visual question → original introduction/disclosure → actual clear-input and messy-input demonstrations → timing/error/control interpretation → research context → limitations/accessibility/privacy → visibly untested hybrid proposal → conclusion and references. No extra AI narrator is introduced.
